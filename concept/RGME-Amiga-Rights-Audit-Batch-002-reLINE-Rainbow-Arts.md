# RGME Amiga Library Rights Audit — Batch 002
## reLINE / Rainbow Arts family

**Date:** 2026-10-28  
**Status:** Evidence-backed research batch; title-specific rights still override publisher-family assumptions.

## Executive findings

This batch deliberately separates **catalogue history** from **rights ownership**. Rainbow Arts was historically connected to many titles, but a later acquisition of part of its catalogue does not prove ownership of every Rainbow Arts release.

A major current rights event is Ziggurat Interactive's acquisition of **more than 80 Rainbow Arts titles**. Public acquisition material explicitly names *Logical*, *Lollypop*, *X-Out* and *M.U.D.S.*. Accordingly, RGME can mark *Logical* and *M.U.D.S.* from this library as high-confidence Ziggurat licensing leads, while other Rainbow Arts games remain title-specific research cases.

*X-Out* is an especially strong proof that the acquired catalogue is being actively modernized: Ziggurat and ININ released *X-Out: Resurfaced*, with Ziggurat asserting copyright/trademark in the modern release.

*Turrican* must remain separate: official modern Turrican collections identify Factor 5 and demonstrate continuing commercial exploitation. RGME must not infer that it belongs to Ziggurat merely because early releases carried Rainbow Arts branding.

Two potentially valuable demo candidates emerged:

- **Fate: Gates of Dawn** — multiple sources report that the creator permitted freeware distribution. The local library contains standard, German and uncensored variants.
- **Mad TV** — historical reporting states that the title became freeware in 2001.

For both, RGME should recover the original permission statement and its platform/version/redistribution scope before treating original binaries as publicly bundleable.

## Rights-family conclusions

### reLINE is not one simple successor bucket

reLINE developed/published titles under several commercial relationships. Later closure, individual authorship and different publishing arrangements mean that the current owner must be reconstructed title by title. The Oil Imperium/Archangels lead from Batch 001 cannot automatically be applied to Fate, Legend of Faerghail, Crystal Hammer or other reLINE titles.

### Rainbow Arts → Ziggurat is important but selective

Ziggurat's acquisition is highly actionable, but the public announcement says **more than 80 titles**, not “all intellectual property ever published by Rainbow Arts”. Only explicitly named titles or title-specific evidence should receive `ziggurat_acquired_catalogue_title`.

### Factor 5 branch

*Turrican*, *Katakis* and *Denaris* deserve a separate rights branch. Modern Turrican exploitation gives a strong current licensing route, but title-specific ownership must still be checked for Katakis/Denaris.

## Recommended RGME priority

1. **Fate: Gates of Dawn** — locate original creator freeware grant; if scope checks out, promote to Class C / GREEN and use as Amiga MVP showcase.
2. **Mad TV** — locate original 2001 freeware statement; verify whether Amiga binary redistribution is covered.
3. **Logical / M.U.D.S.** — direct Ziggurat licensing route.
4. **Oil Imperium** — continue direct chain-of-title outreach from Batch 001.
5. **Turrican** — strong BYOG showcase, but active commercial IP.
6. **Legend of Faerghail** — investigate Electronic Design Hannover/authors plus publishing succession.
7. Remaining reLINE titles — cluster author/company evidence, but keep `research_required` until proven.
